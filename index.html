      Cage Rush

      How to play

      Three rounds of one minute. Win by knockout, TKO (three knockdowns in a round, or the ref stops it), submission, or on the judges' cards.

      ❤️ HealthThe big bar on top. Hit zero and you're out. A little comes back between rounds.
      ⚡ Stamina & guardThe thin yellow bar is stamina, the blue one under it is your guard meter. Every strike costs gas. Gassed fighters punch slow and walk slow. Body shots drain it.
      🧍 Body mapHead, body and legs take damage separately. Wrecked legs slow you down. A wrecked head can get the doctor to stop it.
      🟡 Super meterThe gold bar at the bottom. It fills when you land, block and defend well. It stays full until you use it.

      On a phone

      Left thumb: the stick moves you (flick twice to dash, pull down to block). Right side of the screen is a gesture zone: tap to punch (keep tapping for combos), swipe toward him to kick, swipe up-toward for a head kick, swipe up for a power punch, swipe down to grab, swipe away to slip, and hold to block. Special is the gold button. Prefer buttons? Switch with the Touch button on the title screen or pause menu.

      Combos

      Mash J and it chains. J J K finishes with a head kick. Mixing different strikes in a combo is what really pays: every new strike type adds a big bonus (watch for MIX ×). Throwing the exact same opener three times in a row makes it slow and weak. Combos go up to 2.5×, and 5 in a row puts you ON FIRE. Combos cap at 7 hits, then he gets a chance to escape.

      Up close

      Grab when you're right on top of him to clinch: the camera tightens and it's a different fight. Short punches, knees (to the head once you control him), push him to the cage, take him down, or break off. A tug-of-war bar shows who's in control.

      Time a block right as a body or head kick lands to catch the kick: free shot, dump him, or sweep him. If both of you throw at the same instant you clash and both get knocked back.

      Damage that shows

      Chop the legs and he limps, then can't dash, then his leg gives out. Work the body and he hunches over, his guard gets weaker, and a hard body shot can fold him a beat later. Under 20% health you get desperation haymakers: hold Block + Punch for a slow, huge swing.

      Between rounds

      Your corner gives you a game plan: attack the legs, go to the body, wrestle, headhunt, or recover. The coach recommends one (★) based on how the fight is going.

      Fight smarts

      ✊ Strike › Grab › Block › StrikeHitting someone mid-takedown stuffs it. A grab beats a held block. Blocking beats strikes. Every exchange is a read.
      💨 Whiffs get punishedMiss a big shot and you're stuck for a moment. Hit someone during a whiff for a PUNISH: extra damage and stun.
      ⚡ CountersHit him while he's swinging for a COUNTER. Long stun, big damage. A counter head kick on a hurt fighter is lights out.
      🧱 The cagePin him on the fence: hits bounce him back into you, he stays stunned longer and takedowns are easier.
      🔥 MomentumThe bar under the clock swings toward whoever lands clean. Fill it and the crowd roars: a big chunk of super meter.
      🧠 The AI reads youSpam the same string and harder AI will start blocking and countering it. Mix it up.
      SHold to block. Blocked shots do a fraction of the damage SpaceSlip. Dodge a shot and your next hit is a counter
      🛡️ Perfect blockPress block right as the shot arrives (not held early). Stuns them and gives you meter. Timed right against a grab, it's a sprawl.
      💨 Perfect dodgeSlip at the last moment. Same reward, and their shot whiffs.
      👊 Punch backAfter a perfect block or dodge you get a slow-mo window where your next hit does 1.8× damage.
      🔵 Guard meterThe thin blue bar under stamina fills while you block. When it's full your guard shatters and you're wide open. Don't turtle.

      The gold meter at the bottom fills as you land, block and defend. Use it on O:

      💥 25% · BurstPress O while you're getting hit to blast out of a combo. On the ground it breaks mount and submissions. Back against the cage, it's a cage escape that spins you out.
      ★ 25% · SignaturePress O when you're not getting hit to throw your fighter's signature move: Blaze's Six-Piece, Viper's Switch Kick, Grizzly's German Suplex, Spider's Rolling Kneebar, Hammer's Belly Bump.
      🌟 100% · SuperPress O for your cinematic super. Huge damage. Every style has its own, and you can equip others in the Loadout.
      💀 FinisherWhen he's rocked under 30% health (or getting up from a knockdown), a Finish Window opens: ← → O. No meter needed, and it always ends the fight.
      🌱 Second windDrop under 25% health and you get one free burst.

      Career

      Promotions: win enough fights to earn a title shot, then move up to a bigger promotion with bigger purses and better fighters. At the top you can defend the belt, move up a weight class, or bulk.

      Training & perks: spend money on training camps to raise attributes (your style decides how high each can go) and pick perks as you level.

      Loadout: 3 trait slots and 1 super. Traits are passive boosts (Power Puncher, Iron Chin and more). Buy them with fight money.

      Hype: post, call people out and talk trash. Fans and rivals react, and the story moves with you.
